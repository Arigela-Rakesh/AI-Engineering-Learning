
SAMPLE COMPANY EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK
Version 2.0

IMPORTANT NOTICE
This is fictional sample policy content created for testing a Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) application. It is not an official policy of any real company.

==================================================
1. ANNUAL LEAVE POLICY
==================================================

Employees receive 20 days of annual leave per calendar year.

Annual leave requests should be submitted to the manager at least three working days before the planned leave.

Managers review leave requests based on team availability, project deadlines, and business requirements.

Employees should obtain manager approval before taking planned annual leave.

Unused annual leave may be carried forward for up to five days into the next calendar year, subject to company approval.

Employees should check their available leave balance before submitting a request.

==================================================
2. PRIVILEGE LEAVE POLICY
==================================================

Privilege leave is intended for planned personal time off, vacations, and other personal commitments.

Employees must submit privilege leave requests through the company's designated leave management system.

Privilege leave should normally be requested at least five working days before the planned leave.

Approval depends on the employee's available leave balance, project commitments, and manager approval.

Employees should coordinate with teammates before taking extended privilege leave.

Privilege leave balances and carry-forward rules must be verified through the applicable HR policy.

==================================================
3. SICK LEAVE POLICY
==================================================

Employees who are unable to work because of illness should inform their manager as early as possible.

Employees should record sick leave in the designated leave management system.

For an absence lasting more than two consecutive working days, employees should provide medical documentation if required by the applicable HR policy.

Managers should handle health-related information confidentially.

Employees returning after an extended illness should coordinate with their manager regarding pending work and responsibilities.

The applicable HR policy determines sick leave eligibility and available leave balances.

==================================================
4. CASUAL LEAVE POLICY
==================================================

Casual leave may be requested for urgent personal matters and short-notice commitments.

Employees should inform their manager before taking casual leave whenever reasonably possible.

For unexpected emergencies, employees should notify their manager as soon as practical.

Casual leave requests must be recorded in the designated leave management system.

Approval depends on leave eligibility, applicable HR rules, and business requirements.

Employees should not assume that an unapproved absence has been accepted as casual leave.

==================================================
5. PUBLIC HOLIDAYS AND COMPANY HOLIDAYS
==================================================

The company publishes a holiday calendar for each applicable office location.

Employees should consult the current holiday calendar to confirm official holidays.

Holiday schedules may differ by country, state, and office location.

Employees assigned to critical projects may occasionally be required to support work on a holiday.

Any holiday work arrangements must be discussed with the manager in advance whenever possible.

Compensatory leave or additional payment for holiday work depends on applicable company policy and local law.

==================================================
6. WORK FROM HOME POLICY
==================================================

Employees may work from home up to two days per week, subject to manager approval and team requirements.

Employees must follow their team's agreed working hours while working remotely.

Employees should remain reachable through approved communication tools during working hours.

Employees must protect confidential company information when working outside the office.

Company laptops and other authorized devices should be used according to information security guidelines.

Employees should report technical issues that prevent remote work to the appropriate IT support team.

Working from home does not remove the employee's responsibility to attend required meetings and complete assigned tasks.

==================================================
7. WORKING HOURS AND ATTENDANCE
==================================================

Employees must follow the working hours and attendance requirements applicable to their team and location.

Employees should record attendance using the approved attendance management system.

Employees who expect to arrive late or miss working hours should inform their manager.

Meeting attendance and availability should be coordinated with project requirements.

Flexible working arrangements must be approved by the manager where required.

Repeated attendance issues may be reviewed according to the applicable HR policy.

==================================================
8. INTERNET AND EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT
==================================================

The company reimburses eligible internet expenses up to 1500 rupees per month.

Employees must submit valid bills to the finance team through the approved expense management process.

Expense claims should include the required invoice, transaction details, and business justification where applicable.

Employees should submit reimbursement claims within the deadline specified by the finance policy.

Expenses must be genuine, business-related where required, and supported by valid documentation.

Duplicate, altered, or fraudulent expense claims are prohibited.

The finance team reviews submitted claims and may request additional documentation before approval.

==================================================
9. TRAVEL AND TRANSPORT REIMBURSEMENT
==================================================

Business travel must be approved by the appropriate manager before booking whenever possible.

Employees should use approved travel booking channels where available.

Travel reimbursement claims must include valid receipts and relevant trip details.

Employees should select reasonable transportation and accommodation options within approved budgets.

Personal expenses are not reimbursable unless explicitly permitted by the applicable policy.

Employees should submit travel claims within the deadline established by the finance team.

==================================================
10. ONBOARDING AND TRAINING POLICY
==================================================

New employees must complete onboarding training during their first two weeks.

The manager assigns the required training modules.

Employees should complete mandatory security, compliance, and role-specific training within the assigned deadlines.

Employees should contact their manager or training coordinator if they cannot access a required learning module.

Training completion may be tracked through the company's learning management system.

Employees are encouraged to participate in technical learning and professional development activities.

==================================================
11. INFORMATION SECURITY AND PASSWORDS
==================================================

Employees must protect their company accounts and credentials.

Passwords must not be shared with colleagues or external parties.

Employees should use approved multifactor authentication methods where required.

Suspicious emails, unexpected login prompts, and possible phishing attempts should be reported to the security team.

Employees must not upload confidential company information to unauthorized websites or AI tools.

Company data should be stored and shared only through approved systems.

Employees should lock their devices when leaving their workstations unattended.

==================================================
12. DATA PRIVACY AND CONFIDENTIALITY
==================================================

Employees must handle personal information and confidential business data according to applicable privacy requirements.

Access to company information should be limited to authorized business purposes.

Employees must not disclose confidential customer information to unauthorized people.

Sensitive documents should be shared only through approved channels.

Employees should report suspected data leaks or unauthorized access to the security or privacy team.

Confidentiality obligations may continue after employment ends, subject to applicable agreements and laws.

==================================================
13. IT SUPPORT AND EQUIPMENT
==================================================

Employees should report hardware, software, and network issues through the approved IT service desk.

IT support requests should include a clear description of the problem and any relevant error message.

Employees should not install unauthorized software on company-managed devices.

Company equipment should be used responsibly and protected against loss or damage.

Lost or stolen company devices must be reported to the IT and security teams immediately.

Employees must return company equipment according to the applicable offboarding process.

==================================================
14. PERFORMANCE AND FEEDBACK
==================================================

Employees should discuss goals, priorities, and performance expectations with their managers.

Managers may conduct regular feedback discussions to review progress and identify development opportunities.

Employees should communicate project risks and delivery delays as early as possible.

Performance evaluations should follow the company's established review process.

Employees are encouraged to document achievements, completed training, and professional development activities.

Promotion and compensation decisions depend on the applicable company process and eligibility criteria.

==================================================
15. CODE OF CONDUCT AND PROFESSIONAL BEHAVIOR
==================================================

Employees are expected to behave respectfully and professionally toward colleagues, customers, and business partners.

Harassment, discrimination, bullying, and retaliation are not acceptable.

Employees should report serious workplace concerns through the designated HR or ethics reporting channels.

Employees must disclose potential conflicts of interest according to company requirements.

Company resources should be used responsibly and for authorized purposes.

Employees are expected to follow applicable laws, company rules, and professional standards.

==================================================
16. MATERNITY, PATERNITY, AND PARENTAL LEAVE
==================================================

Employees should contact HR to understand parental leave eligibility and application procedures.

Parental leave entitlements depend on the employee's work location, applicable law, and the official company policy.

Employees should submit the required documentation through the designated HR process.

Employees are encouraged to discuss planned leave and handover arrangements with their managers.

This sample handbook does not specify a fixed number of maternity or paternity leave days.

Employees must consult HR for the applicable entitlement and approval requirements.

==================================================
17. RESIGNATION AND OFFBOARDING
==================================================

Employees who plan to resign should submit their resignation through the approved company process.

Notice periods and separation requirements are determined by the employment agreement and applicable company policy.

Employees should coordinate knowledge transfer and pending work with their managers.

Company equipment, access cards, and other assigned assets must be returned during offboarding.

Employees should complete required exit formalities and expense settlements.

System access is handled according to the company's security and offboarding procedures.

==================================================
18. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
==================================================

Question: How many annual leave days are provided?
Answer: Employees receive 20 days of annual leave per calendar year.

Question: How much internet reimbursement is available?
Answer: Eligible internet expenses can be reimbursed up to 1500 rupees per month, subject to the applicable process.

Question: How many days per week can employees work from home?
Answer: Employees may work from home up to two days per week, subject to manager approval and team requirements.

Question: When should new employees complete onboarding training?
Answer: New employees must complete onboarding training during their first two weeks.

Question: Is there a fixed number of maternity leave days in this handbook?
Answer: No fixed number is specified. Employees must consult HR for the applicable entitlement.

Question: Can employees share their passwords?
Answer: No. Employees must not share their passwords with colleagues or external parties.

Question: Who approves business travel?
Answer: The appropriate manager must approve business travel before booking whenever possible.

Question: Where should employees report phishing emails?
Answer: Employees should report suspicious emails and possible phishing attempts to the security team.

Question: What should employees do if company equipment is stolen?
Answer: Employees must report lost or stolen company devices to the IT and security teams immediately.

Question: How can employees confirm official holidays?
Answer: Employees should consult the current holiday calendar for their office location.
